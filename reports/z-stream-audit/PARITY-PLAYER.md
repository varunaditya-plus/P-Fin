# Retained player feature comparison

Primary visual/behaviour source is the **deployed pstream.cfd build** downloaded to `/tmp/movie-fin-parity/reference-assets` on this run. The local `references/p-stream-xp-technologies` checkout is a secondary source: several older components differ from the deployed site. Root is carrying out browser verification separately. Source review and automated checks do not establish hardware behaviour.
